Evidence Base

Clinical Background & Methodology

Why traditional Type 2 diabetes classifications fall short and how sub-phenotyping transforms patient care.

Beyond the Type 2 Monolith

Type 2 diabetes represents a highly heterogeneous syndrome. Patients with identical HbA1c levels often have vastly divergent underlying pathophysiologies: from autoimmune beta-cell failure to extreme insulin resistance or isolated adiposity.

Complication Prevention

SIRD patients face a 3x higher risk of chronic kidney disease and non-alcoholic fatty liver, requiring early organ-protective SGLT2i and GLP-1 RAs, whereas SIDD patients exhibit accelerated diabetic retinopathy and early insulin requirement.

The HOMA-IR Foundation

Incorporating Fasting Insulin and Fasting Glucose to derive Homeostatic Model Assessment of Insulin Resistance (HOMA-IR) unlocks critical mechanistic distinction between secretory defect and receptor insensitivity.
